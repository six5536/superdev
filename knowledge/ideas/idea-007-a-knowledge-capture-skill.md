# Idea: a knowledge-capture skill

A skill that teaches an agent when and how to record a durable learning in
the canonical knowledge mid-task: which concept the learning belongs in,
what links it carries, and which sources it cites. The write-side complement
to the search-first `AGENTS.md`, which says how to read the knowledge and
nothing about how to add to it.

## Motivation

Judged a good idea during the skill-pack design and deferred to the
knowledge-upkeep sub-project. Recorded here on the backlog's retirement
(ADR-048), where it sat under consideration.
