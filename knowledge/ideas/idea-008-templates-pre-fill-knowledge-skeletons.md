# Idea: templates pre-fill knowledge skeletons

A project template fixes facts about the repository it creates — the
rust-npm scaffold establishes the Rust workspace, the npm launcher and the
CI shape — so it could pre-fill parts of the `technology-stack` and
`architecture` skeletons instead of leaving them `TBD`, shrinking
`bootstrap`'s interview to the genuinely human questions.

## Motivation

Raised while designing the bootstrap interview phase. Recorded here on the
backlog's retirement (ADR-048), where it sat under consideration.
